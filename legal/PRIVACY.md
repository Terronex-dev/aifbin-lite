# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** February 2, 2026  
**Last Updated:** February 2, 2026

## TL;DR

AIF-BIN Lite collects **zero data**. Everything runs locally on your machine.

---

## Data Collection

AIF-BIN Lite does **not** collect, transmit, or store any data:

- ❌ No analytics or telemetry
- ❌ No network requests
- ❌ No crash reporting
- ❌ No usage tracking
- ❌ No cookies or identifiers

## How It Works

AIF-BIN Lite is a local command-line tool. When you run it:

1. It reads files from your local filesystem
2. It processes them entirely on your machine
3. It writes output files to your local filesystem

No data ever leaves your computer.

## Your Files

- Files you process remain **100% on your device**
- No data is sent to Terronex.dev or any third party
- You have full ownership and control of all files

## Third-Party Services

AIF-BIN Lite does not integrate with any third-party services.

**Note:** AIF-BIN Pro (the commercial product) may optionally use third-party embedding APIs if you configure them. That usage is covered by Pro's separate privacy policy.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be noted by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
